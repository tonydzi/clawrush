### ⭐ Channel sizes, which invert the premise

Live figures from the Telegram API on this node:

|---|---|---|

| `@PaloAltoAiRu` (RU) | **3,431** | **no** |

Against the figure from the 21 October community post, roughly 100 registered members with 10 to 20 attending meetups, the problem is not a missing audience. The English channel alone is three orders of magnitude larger than the community. There is an audience and no community, which is the exact complaint of the 3 November post, now with a denominator.

### ⛔ Hard blocker: the RU channel has no comment button

The brief's method is to ask, "guys, what are you doing, what are you designing?". `@PaloAltoAiRu` has **no linked discussion chat**, so readers there have no way to reply, and this has been the case continuously. Inviting 3,431 subscribers into a room and asking them a question they physically cannot answer is a wiring defect, not a content one. The English side is wired (`@ClawEng` is the linked chat for `@PaloAltoAi`).

⚠️ Note the standing decision this interacts with: RU discussions were proposed and **declined** earlier, deliberately, to avoid opening a second moderation stream. So this is a live trade-off rather than an oversight, and it needs a decision rather than a fix: either wire RU discussions and accept the moderation load, or stop writing briefs whose method requires replies in a channel that cannot take them.

### The cleaning half already has a tool and a rule

"Clean out the unnecessary messages" maps to the existing thread-cleaning path. The rule is narrow on purpose: our own fan-out and unanswered volleys get cleared from a DM before a new touch; **the lead's own messages are never deleted**; deletion is irreversible, so a human presses it. Worth restating here because the brief's phrasing ("clean unnecessary messages") is broader than the rule allows.

### Open, in order of payoff

1. Decide the RU comment button before any invitation goes out. The method of the brief depends on it, and the standing decision says no, so this is a decision to revisit, not a task to do.
2. Read `DR26-08-29-MACANTON-16-0743` rather than re-commissioning it. It answers the "show them how I use Codex and Cursor" half directly.
3. Instrument the decay, not the restart. September proved a restart works; nothing explains why it stopped on 2 October. An age gate on the ledger (N days at zero turns red and reaches a human) is the smallest thing that would have caught it.
4. Reconcile the audience numbers. 147,406 subscribers against about 100 community members is the actual conversion problem, and no brief so far names it.
5. Apply the July selection memo for "whoever reacts fastest" instead of leaving the choice to intuition at send time.
