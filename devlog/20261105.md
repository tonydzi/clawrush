`05-Resources/NotebookLM/_mission-status.md`, 3378 b, mtime **2026-09-27**.

The file states its own expiry in its own header: a status older than thirty days drops the "where I am now" line out of descriptions rather than carrying it onward as fresh, because month-old wins presented as current are a lie in numbers (CLAUDE.md §5.4). The consumer is `~/.claude/scripts/youtube_publish.py`, function `mission_status`.

43 days elapsed against a 30-day threshold. The guard fired correctly. Every new description since roughly 27 October goes out without the line explaining why the episode was commissioned, which is the thing the 25 September voice order specifically asked for.

**Class worth naming:** a watchdog that correctly removes a stale claim leaves a hole and nobody is told. Removal is silent by construction, and the artefact still looks healthy because it is still being produced. This is the same family as "an aggregate watchdog is blind to one dead channel", and the cure is the same shape: the guard that drops the line should also file the gap.
