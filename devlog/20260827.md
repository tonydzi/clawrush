# 27 August 2026 - dev log

Engineering events of the day, for machines and for whoever reads logs.

## Finite routines: the shape

A long enumerable job (thousands of chats, years of watch history, tens of thousands of files) is not a task. Two failure modes if you treat it as one:

1. One heroic session dies on a rate limit or a vendor cap, and the work is lost with it.
2. A naive daily cron runs forever, because nothing tells it the queue is empty.

The shape that works has three parts: a durable queue of chunks, a per-chunk failure counter, and a terminal condition. A chunk that has failed three times is dropped from the queue, otherwise the elephant never ends. When the queue hits zero the routine disables its own scheduled task and reports.

**The trap, measured on this fleet:** the progress journal must never live in a synced folder. On one node the journal sat inside the Syncthing transit directory. 453 runs produced 39 lines: the routine chewed the same chunks for 19 days and logged success every time. The journal was being overwritten by sync before the next run read it.

## Content pipeline

Twelve placements across three cases. Chain state is per-case JSON, and every step records its own URL and timestamp, which is what makes a truthful audit possible later.

Platform ceilings enforced by the engine, not by memory:
- X counts every link as exactly 23 characters regardless of real length. A 106-character GitHub URL costs 23. The counter has to model this or it rejects valid text.
- Threads counts the real length, so links are moved below a marker into the first comment and only the body counts against the 500 limit.

## Value gate on teasers

Every teaser is checked for a carrier of value: a number with context, a measurement, a runnable command, an invariant rule, or a named antipattern. A teaser that only promises value behind a link fails. Two rewrites today were caused by this gate, both correctly.

## CRM ingestion, design note

Parsing group members into a CRM: the summary step needs the surrounding context, not just the message. A bare "yes yes" carries no information without the question it answered. Store the neighbourhood, not the line.
