## Post 1: Codex tier comparison - the official numbers do not exist, and the local bucket is 16% used

Source post (18 November, 03:10): claim under test is "the $500 Codex subscription is better value than several at $200". He states he cannot see the advantage and may conclude there is none. Task as stated: re-read what the $200 tier includes versus the $500 tier, compare terms, decide.

Method: official vendor pages first (chatgpt.com pricing, chatgpt.com/codex/pricing, OpenAI help article on using Codex with a ChatGPT plan), third-party measurement tables second, our own registries and instruments third. Browser read, no login required for the pricing pages; the help-centre article was fetched through the in-app browser after a direct fetch returned HTTP 403.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (a) The vendor publishes no Pro-tier limits at all

`chatgpt.com/ru-RU/codex/pricing/` carries a table titled "Ограничения использования" with three columns only:

| model | Plus | Standard Business | API key |
|---|---|---|---|
| GPT-6.1 Sol | 15-160 | 15-160 | pay-as-you-go |
| GPT-6 Astra | 5-45 | 5-45 | pay-as-you-go |
| GPT-6 Sol | 15-150 | 15-150 | pay-as-you-go |
| GPT-6 Luna | 350-3,000 | 350-3,000 | pay-as-you-go |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | 10-100 | 10-100 | pay-as-you-go |
| GPT-5.6 Luna | 250-2,000 | 250-2,000 | pay-as-you-go |
| GPT-5.5 | 15-80 | 15-80 | pay-as-you-go |

No Pro column exists. The plan page says only "your choice of 3 usage levels" and "maximum number of Codex tasks". Help article 11369540 (updated ~15 days before this reading) says "Codex is included across ChatGPT plans... Usage limits vary by plan" and refers the reader to the pricing page, i.e. a loop.

**Consequence for the task as stated:** "compare what is in the $200 and what is in the $500" cannot be completed against the vendor. Any comparison is third-party-sourced by construction, and must be labelled as such.

Two official facts from the same pages that do bear on the decision:
- "Local messages and cloud chats draw on your plan's shared limit. Weekly limits may also apply." - one account means local and cloud contend for one allowance.
- Auto-review safety checks, when signed in with a ChatGPT account, "are free and don't count toward your plan's usage limits."
- FAQ line: GPT-6 Astra is included in the existing Codex allowance "on Pro $100 and $200 plans and Business $100". Pro 500 is not named in that sentence; most likely an incomplete list rather than an exclusion, so no conclusion drawn.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (b) Only official per-tier differentiator: API credits, and $200 leads per dollar

From the official pricing page feature table, row "Monthly API credits":

| tier | credits/month | per $ paid |
|---|---|---|
| Pro 100 | 103 € | 1.03 |
| **Pro 200** | **229 €** | **1.15** |
| Pro 500 | 510 € | 1.02 |

Free / Go / Plus: none. Credits expire at the end of the billing period; activation conditions apply; the grant is prorated on first issue and OpenAI reserves the right to change or withdraw it.

Basket comparison: 2 × Pro 200 = $400 paid / 458 € credits. 1 × Pro 500 = $500 paid / 510 € credits.

⚠️ Locale: the page served euro pricing (IP-based; forcing `/en-US/pricing/` redirected back to `ru-RU`). Absolute values are euro-denominated, ratios are not affected.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (c) Third-party quota table: price per unit is flat across the ladder

Most detailed table found (updated 2026-11-03) gives Pro multipliers vs Plus, and states they were doubled in November 2026 from 5x/10x/25x to 10x/20x/50x:

| tier | $/mo | xPlus | quota per $ |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus | 20 | 1x | 0.050 |
| Pro 100 | 100 | 10x | 0.100 |
| Pro 200 | 200 | 20x | 0.100 |
| Pro 500 | 500 | 50x | 0.100 |

Flat 0.100 across all three Pro tiers: no volume discount exists in the ladder. The only step that changes the rate is Plus → Pro, which doubles it.

Same source: the 5-hour window is rolling, starts at first request in a cycle, counted per model; Cloud Chats and Local Messages share it; the Codex App client is reported to get double the quota of CLI and web on the same plan; Fast mode "doubles quota consumption". No official weekly figure; community estimate ~10-15 full 5-hour cycles per 7 days at maximum intensity.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (d) The $500-exclusive feature inverts the comparison

Ultrafast is reported as the only personal-tier-exclusive feature of Pro 500, drawing included usage at **8x** the Standard rate (Fast = 2.5x).

| configuration | effective Plus-units |
|---|---|
| Pro 200, Standard | 20 |
| Pro 500, Standard | 50 |
| **Pro 500, Ultrafast** | **6.25** |

So the tier bought for the exclusive mode, used in that mode, yields less working quota than the tier below it in Standard. Throughput is purchased out of the same allowance, not alongside it.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (e) Multi-account is a mechanism, not just a price

A single subscription exposes one bucket and one reset clock. N subscriptions expose N of each, and that is machine-exploitable. Already live on the Anthropic rail per `~/.claude/llm_routing.json → claude_account_ladder`: three Claude Max accounts (a / a2 / bb), contract text *"an empty weekly bucket on account #1 = the next token on the ladder (no hands needed, no re-authorisation), and only when the ladder is exhausted does the vendor change per `_failover_policy`"*. Token home: `<SECRETS_DIR>/claude_headless_<acct>.env`, bound to the account rather than the machine; readers are `claude_token_ladder.py → claude_run.py`.

No equivalent ladder exists for the codex rail: the registry holds a single `codex` rail. Artefacts that would support one are present (`_test_codex_token_pull.py`, `_chatgpt_token_fleet.jsonl`, `chatgpt_bearer_a2.txt`), so this is a gap rather than an impossibility. Not built, not proposed here.

⚠️ Third-party reporting says ChatGPT and Codex allowances will be pooled into one shared allowance from December across Plus, Pro and Business, counted in tokens, with instant chat exempt and combined usage shown in Settings → Usage. OpenAI help pages did not carry this as of 2026-11-03. If true, pooling penalises the single-large-account shape further.

### ⚠️ (g) Disagreements and instrument defects, named

1. **Multipliers.** 10x/20x/50x (detailed table) vs "25x Plus" for Pro 500 (press, citing OpenAI pre-29 October). One page self-contradicts: Pro 100 described as both 10x and "5x the full quota". Unverified.
2. **5-hour window on Pro.** One source: Pro tiers have no 5-hour Codex limit, weekly only. Another: publishes per-5-hour ranges for Pro 100/200/500. Mutually exclusive.
3. **$200 allowance history.** Conflicting reports on whether a planned reduction was cancelled or all $200 subscriptions were levelled up on 29 October. No official statement.
4. **Our own registry vs our own instrument.** `llm_routing.json` records the codex rail as `plan: ChatGPT Pro, usd_month: 200`; `subscription_utilization.py report` prints the chatgpt row at `$20`. One is stale. Not reconciled in this pass, and the utilisation percentage is therefore a claim about a bucket whose size our own two sources describe differently.
5. **Age of the local reading.** The 16% figure is from 27 October, 22 days before this writing. It is the freshest machine reading available; no reading exists for November.

### Open, in order of payoff

1. **Reconcile the two local records of the chatgpt plan** ($20 vs $200). Until then every utilisation percentage on that rail is measured against an ambiguous denominator.
2. **Take a fresh bucket reading** (`subscription_utilization.py codex`) and three panel readings for claude / grok / gemini. Three of four rails are unmeasured, so "we underuse our subscriptions" is currently supported by one line out of four.
3. **Decide the tier question with the arithmetic above, not with the forum consensus.** Per unit of quota the ladder is flat; per API credit the $200 leads; the $500-only mode costs 8x per call.
4. **If a second ChatGPT account is wanted, build the ladder first.** Multiple accounts pay off through automated stepping, which exists for Claude and does not exist for codex. Without the ladder, a second account is a second thing to switch by hand.
5. **Re-check in December** whether the pooled ChatGPT+Codex allowance ships. It changes the shape of the answer: pooling makes several smaller buckets strictly better than one large one.
