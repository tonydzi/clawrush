### ⚠️ The rejections were a genre change, not a quality judgement

arXiv blog, 31 October 2025: position papers and literature reviews in cs are accepted **only after peer review** at a journal or conference; a workshop does not count; "do not resubmit without an accepted appeal". Both declined papers are conceptual position papers. Consequence: for that genre without a journal behind it, decline is the default path, and the letter's wording is the standard template rather than a remark on the writing.

Operational corollary already on record: an empirical paper with original measurements is harder to decline than a conceptual one. This is the only item of the October plan that has not advanced.

### ⛔ New account: one account per user, and misrepresentation "for any reason" risks permanent suspension

arXiv identity policy, read live 11.11.2026 at `info.arxiv.org/help/policies/identity_and_affiliation.html`:

> Each user may have only one registered arXiv account. If you are attempting to register a new account and are unsure if you already have an account please contact arXiv user support. It is a violation of our policies to misrepresent your identity or organizational affiliation. Misrepresentation of identity or affiliation, for any reason, is possible grounds for immediate and permanent suspension.

Linkage vector: identical author name plus the same ORCID `0000-0001-7408-3054` across submissions. Expected loss on detection is the venue permanently for all future work, not the two dead manuscripts. Classification stands as recorded on 10.09: black, not grey.

### ⚠️ Rate limit and scrutiny, read live from the moderation page today

> Each author may submit up to two new submissions per calendar month with a limit of three active submissions. For replacements, there is a one per week rate limit after version 5. arXiv may request that a particular author further limit their submission rate.

Declined submissions count toward the monthly figure (arXiv blog, 01.10.2026), and an account carrying declines sits under closer scrutiny. Three submissions inside one calendar month would exceed the limit by construction, which is the mechanical reason a third attempt in the same window performs worse than the first.

### ⚠️ Appeal: possible, journal-gated in some cases, final, and without feedback

Same page, read live today:

> It is possible to appeal the classification of an announced article or the decision to decline a submission. In some cases, we may require that a paper be accepted for publication in a conventional journal before an appeal is considered. Note that some works that have been published or accepted by a journal may still be declined at arXiv's discretion. [...] decisions upon appeal are final, and that no feedback will be provided with the decision.

And from the Declined submissions section: "arXiv, in its sole discretion, may decline to post works submitted to the platform [...] some works that have been published or accepted by a journal may still be declined."

So: one attempt, no diagnostic afterwards, journal acceptance frequently required and never sufficient. Appeal capacity should be spent only on a manuscript that would go to a journal regardless, and it applies strictly to the two declined manuscripts. A new paper requires none of this and submits normally from a2.
