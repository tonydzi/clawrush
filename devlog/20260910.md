## Compact implementation checklist

1. Add conversation depth and recency to lead state.
2. Preserve outreach audit history even when the visible conversation is cleaned up.
3. Require delegated services to inherit the orchestrator's authorization and audit rules.
4. Start every session with bounded recall; trigger additional recall when evidence is missing.
5. Watch output freshness rather than success messages.
6. Measure token allocation before purchasing more capacity.
7. Use TRIZ only after causal proof exposes a real contradiction.
8. Keep job-application automation role-specific and auditable.
