## Post 1: Argon vs Carbon - 9 index points apart, tied on Arena Text, same token price, and his eval hit the tie

Source: Anton's post of 23 November 2026, 00:05 UTC. Claim under test: "both are positioned like Astra at ChatGPT and like Fable; what is the real difference? A riddle." Cross-reference: his post of 20 November 10:10 UTC reported an eval across both models on "classic tasks, same skill set, same inputs" with no noticeable difference.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (a) The intelligence gap is real and measurable

| metric | Argon | Carbon |
|---|---|---|
| Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v5.0 | ~**50** | ~**59-60** |

A 9-10 point spread, roughly 20% relative. So the premise "both positioned at the same tier" is marketing-true and benchmark-false.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (b) The gain is uneven, and this explains his null eval result

| benchmark | delta (Carbon over Argon) |
|---|---|
| GraphWalks BFS F1 | **+12.0 points** |
| SWE-sweep | **+5.3 pp** |
| coding, cybersecurity suites | Carbon ahead |
| **Arena Text** | **tied** - gap inside published rating ranges |

His eval ran "classic tasks" with identical skill sets and inputs and found no difference. That is consistent with the published data rather than contradicting it: on general conversational work the two models are statistically indistinguishable. The spread lives in multi-step code repair, graph/dependency traversal and security reasoning.

**Actionable:** an eval that wants to separate these two must load on SWE-style repair or graph traversal. A chat-shaped eval will return "no difference" by construction.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (c) Token price identical; second-order economics differ and conflict

| dimension | Argon | Carbon |
|---|---|---|
| input, until 30 Nov 2026 | $2 / M | $2 / M |
| output, until 30 Nov 2026 | $10 / M | $10 / M |
| input / output from 1 Dec 2026 | **$4 / $20** | **$4 / $20** |
| cost per task (Artificial Analysis) | $9.95 (high effort) | **$7.76** |
| output tokens across full index | 113 M | **205 M** |
| time to first token, high effort | **37.44 s** | 66.35 s |

So: Carbon costs less per *completed task* while emitting ~1.8x the output tokens, and Argon starts answering ~29 s sooner. Nothing in the price list distinguishes them; the distinguishing numbers are task-completion cost, verbosity and latency.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (d) ⚠️ Access: the secondary record is self-contradictory

Three claims found, mutually incompatible:

1. Argon open via paid API; **Carbon** gated behind Google's **Fairwind Program** (trusted cyber defenders only).
2. **Argon** gated behind Fairwind; paid API and Google AI Ultra subscribers "next in line", no date published.
3. Google's own Fairwind pages name **Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber** as the program's model, and are dated **2 September** - 28 days before Argon's 30 September announcement.

Fairwind participation requirements (per Google's FAQ): contact your Cloud account team; agree to user-level authentication, phishing-resistant MFA and applicable access controls.

⚠️ Do not resolve this from secondary sources. The one hard data point available: **the author ran an eval across both models through Antigravity on 20 November**, so both were reachable from his environment on that date. That supersedes press claims about general unavailability *for his case only*.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (e) The line has a third member

His "Argon, Carbon and so on" is literal: comparison write-ups of this generation cover **Argon, Carbon and Hydrogen**. Any model-selection logic in a harness should enumerate three, not two.

### Open, in order of payoff (post 1)

1. **Re-run the eval on the right axis.** The existing eval measured the tie. Load it on SWE-style repair and graph traversal to see the 5-12 point spread, or stop expecting a difference.
2. **Pick by shape of work, not by price.** Identical per-token cost; choose Carbon for code/security depth, Argon for latency and output economy.
3. **Diary the 1 December price change.** Both models double: $2/$10 → $4/$20. Any routing decision made on current prices expires in 8 days.
4. **Record which models are actually reachable from which account.** The public record cannot answer it; the environment can, and that answer is the only one that matters for routing.
5. **Add Hydrogen to the routing table** or state explicitly that it is excluded, with a reason.
