## Post 2: "hooks fire only Thu/Fri/Sat, only on sessions >300 KB" - both conditions measured false; the brake is one `PreToolUse` gate doing 2 synchronous Syncthing REST calls per edit (1.93s of a 2.32s chain), costing ~7 hours per week on this node alone

**The claim.** Verbatim from the note: hooks slow a session down once it has gone stale; they fire only on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, and only on sessions over 300 kilobytes; the lag is constant and needs optimising on the Mac Studio.

**(a) Weekday condition: false, and the named day is the quietest.** Counted from `~/.claude/hooks/_receiveonly_edit_guard.log`, n=**4,999** dated lines, 2026-07-30 → 2026-11-26 (119 days): Thu **1,285** (26%) · Mon **1,017** (20%) · Fri **854** (17%) · Wed **796** (16%) · Tue **755** (15%) · Sun **203** (4%) · **Sat 89 (1.8%)**. Cross-checked against `_no_silent_wait.log`, n=**5,834**, same window: Thu 1,235 · Wed 1,216 · Tue 1,141 · Mon 1,110 · Fri 760 · Sun 220 · **Sat 152 (2.6%)**. Every weekday is present in both. Saturday, named as one of three active days, is the minimum in both logs.

**(b) No day-of-week condition exists anywhere in the config.** `grep -rE "weekday\(\)\s*(in|==|>=|<=)|isoweekday\(\)\s*(in|==)"` over `~/.claude/hooks` + `~/.claude/scripts` (`*.py`) returns exactly **one** file: `scripts/health_watchdog.py`, function `_is_monday()` → `datetime.date.today().weekday() == 0`. It is Monday, not Thu/Fri/Sat, and `grep -c health_watchdog settings.json` = **0**, i.e. it is not wired as a hook at all. Inside `hooks/*.py` and `settings.json`: zero matches for `weekday`/`isoweekday`.

**(c) 300 KB threshold: not a filter.** `~/.claude/projects/*/*.jsonl`, n=**1,070** transcripts: **967 (90.4%)** exceed 300 KiB (970 exceed 300,000 bytes). Median **1,661.8 KB**, p90 **5,697.5 KB**, max **433.7 MB**. A threshold at 300 KB excludes the shortest ~10% of sessions. "Only over 300 KB" describes nine out of ten.

**(d) Hook inventory on this node.** `settings.json` → 36 hooks: `PreToolUse` **17** · `SessionStart` **10** · `Stop` **4** · `PostToolUse` **3** · `UserPromptSubmit` **1** · `WorktreeCreate` **1**. The 17 are the cost driver: they fire per tool call, not per session. Matchers: 8 on `Bash|PowerShell`, 4 on `Write|Edit|MultiEdit|NotebookEdit`, the rest on specific MCP tools.

**(e) Measured chains (cold single runs, stdin = a real PreToolUse payload).**

| chain | hooks | total | slowest member |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Bash`/`PowerShell` | 8 | **0.81 s** | `blackbox_session_guard` 0.15 s |
| `Write`/`Edit` | 4 | **2.32 s** | **`receiveonly_edit_guard` 1.93 s (83%)** |

Component split for the 1.93 s: bare interpreter start **0.032 s**; one live `GET http://127.0.0.1:8384/rest/system/status` with the API key from `config.xml` → **0.453 s**, 2,804 bytes. The gate makes two REST calls (`/rest/config/folders`, then a per-path lookup) plus an `ET.parse` of `config.xml`, so ~0.9-1.4 s is network wait inside a loopback call. `TIMEOUT` defaults to 3 s (`RECVONLY_GUARD_TIMEOUT`), so this is not a timeout - it is a slow success. Its own docstring records "Syncthing (REST, ~30 ms)" measured 2026-07-29 on Mac16: **64x faster than this node**. Log verdicts: ALLOW 4,747 · FAILOPEN 147 (2.9%, all `URLError: timed out`) · BLOCK **66** · other 40. The gate is doing real work - it has blocked 66 edits that would have been reverted by sync - the cost is in how it asks.
