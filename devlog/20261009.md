## The protection half of pedagogy is missing from ML, and it is missing because it needs an instrument

**The request:** a deep research pass on whether health-preserving education research transfers to the training of ML models.

**The finding, as a clean split rather than a verdict.** Borrowed already, under ML's own names: curriculum learning, self-paced learning, BabyLM, spaced repetition. Not borrowed: diagnostics of the learning state, recovery phases triggered by threshold instead of schedule, ergonomics of the form of the material, accountability traced over a concept graph.

**Why that split is not arbitrary.** Everything in the first list is a *schedule*: an ordering of material, computable before the run starts. Everything in the second list requires *reading the state of the learner mid-process* and acting on it. The first half transfers by writing a curriculum function; the second half transfers only if you first build the instrument that measures damage. That is the engineering reason the second half is still empty, and it generalises past this subject: the mechanisms that get copied are the ones that do not need a sensor.

**Candidate instrument, proposed not validated:** loss instability, gradient instability, forgetting, representation damage, calibration loss, plasticity loss, recovery depth. Treat as a list of signals to try, not a metric suite. None of these has been wired to a recovery trigger in our work.

**Transferable rule:** a recovery phase fired by a measured threshold is strictly more informative than one fired by a calendar, because the calendar version cannot tell "not needed yet" from "needed and missed". Applies to training runs and to human schedules alike. Cost: you must instrument the state first, which is the whole work.

**Prior art, stated carefully:** direct papers applying the source research to ML training came back at zero in this pass. An empty result is evidence about the search as well as about the field; before building on it, the search terms are the thing to attack.

**Funding surfaces recorded:** OpenAI Development Grant and Research Grant, Foresight AI Safety Grants, Emerging Ventures.

**Measured on the human side:** research runs put a child's effective study ceiling at 6 hours; the school day in question runs 8:30 to roughly 4. That gap is what started the whole line of inquiry.

**Not measured:** any effect of any of this on a model. No experiment, no baseline, no run.

**Open item:** the next step is not a grant application, it is one instrument. Pick a single signal from the list above, wire it to a threshold, and see whether a recovery phase fired by it beats the same phase fired on a schedule. Without that, the whole subject stays a well-named hypothesis.
