# A registry of all my routines, so none of them dies in silence

Let me tell you about one more thing I have. A whole pile of automations and robots run across my machines. Some every hour, some at night, some watch the mail, some watch the sync, and so on. And at some point I realized I no longer remember them all. That is a real risk for the whole process. A robot can quietly die and I would never know. It just stopped working, and you go on thinking everything is running.

So I set up a REGISTRY of all my routines. One single list: what the robot is, which machine it runs on, what it does, how often, and how to tell it actually ran, not just that it is nominally switched on. The idea is simple: until a given process and its automation are in the registry, it is as if they do not exist. It might be running fine, it might not be running at all, you do not know. But the moment it lands in the registry, it is in plain view, it has an owner, and it has a check. This gave me peace of mind. I no longer hold thirty robots in my head. I look at one list and see what is alive and what went quiet. And I fix it not when it is already too late, but right away.
