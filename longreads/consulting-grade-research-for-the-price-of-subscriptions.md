# Consulting-grade research for the price of three AI subscriptions

*The disagreement between the reports is worth more than any single report.*

---

Here is the trick that replaced a junior analyst for me: every strategic question goes through Deep Research in three different models at once, and then I make their reports collide.

## The mechanic, plainly

ChatGPT, Gemini and Grok all have Deep Research: the model digs the web for 10 to 40 minutes and writes a report with sources. It is included in the ordinary subscriptions.

My agent does this:

1. The same question goes to all three services simultaneously. The rule is hard: the request text must be word-for-word identical, otherwise comparing the reports turns into mush.
2. Half an hour later the agent collects three reports and builds a table: where they agreed, where they diverged, what only one of them found.
3. "Three out of three agree" I read as working truth. "They diverged" I read as a fork where I have to think for myself.

## A recent example with numbers

Last week I asked: what voice should I write in on the big Russian tech platforms so I do not sound like a language model. Three reports came back — 90 sources in one, 32 thousand characters of analysis in another.

The valuable part was in the disagreement. All three produced a list of "top authors on the platform," and the lists **barely overlapped**. Each one sounded confident on its own; side by side it became obvious that these are three different windows into reality, and none of them can be trusted blindly. Without the fan-out I would have taken the first list as fact.

That is the whole argument for this method in one observation. A single report gives you an answer. Three reports give you a measurement of how much that answer is worth.

## The traps, honestly

**Quotas are expensive.** My agent is not allowed to burn a run until it has proven the request was pasted in full and the right mode was on. Before that rule I was losing runs to "it half-sent."

**One of the services sometimes phones it in:** a report in six minutes without a single trip to the web. We mark those as "opinion" rather than "research," and they carry less weight.

**Links need spot-checking.** One of the models likes inventing beautiful sources. It takes a minute to catch: open three random links.

## What to take for yourself

- A strategic question deserves at least two independent studies. The divergence between reports is more valuable than either of them alone.
- Demand a confidence marker on every conclusion and an honest "source not found." That is one line in your request, and it cuts the garbage in the report several times over.
- Do the economics: three subscriptions cost about one hour of a consultant, and they produce dozens of studies a month.
