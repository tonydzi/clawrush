## What went out today

### A voice message should arrive with everything I said before it

I want to rework, together with my Claude, how my voice messages are handled.

A new voice message lands in the chat and gets transcribed automatically. Right now that is roughly where the understanding stops. What I want is for the session to take that message and look back: through the history of all my voice messages, and straight into the vault, at what I have said on this topic before and what the latest tasks were.

If the message is about, say, the construction, it should find everything I have said about the construction. Several earlier voice notes on the same topic, my latest one, maybe older messages too. From that it understands the context.

Only after it has a lot of context on that specific topic should my robot set the task. For example, a purchase: forward it to the purchases chat together with the context.

So the real job is to enrich the context. And that is only possible if the robot looks at what has already been done on this topic, what already happened. For that, all my messages have to be checked.

Which brings me to the part I need to verify. In my setup every message is supposed to land in the vault. So I need to make sure that everything in the purchases chat, the construction chat and the assistants chat really is in the vault, not forgotten, indexed, reindexed, relinked and easy to find.

### Deep research across 6 LLMs: a quorum of 4, the rest collected later

I need to finish and improve my deep research skill.

Every deep research I do goes to 6 LLMs. The models and accounts I have: GLM, Mistral AI, Grok, Gemini AI, Perplexity (2 accounts), ChatGPT (2 accounts), Claude (3 accounts). Once I decide to run one, I send it to as many places as I can, and never to fewer than 3 different models. Each one runs in its strongest thinking mode: the longest, most detailed reasoning it offers. Every LLM switches that on differently, and the strongest mode has to be picked every time.

The quorum is 4 of 6. When 4 reports are in, I can move on without waiting for the other two. If all 6 arrive, even better, but the quorum is the minimum. The two that are late are not lost: a robot goes around my LLM accounts once a day and collects every research report that arrived after the session ended.

Then the research has to become content, right away. The post is teaser length, about 230-240 characters, and it gives a piece of value on its own: for anyone interested in this topic, I ran a deep research across 4 of 6 LLMs on this question, and here is the short verdict. The research reports, the synthesis and the references go into the comments, with links to where people can take them.

Two more things matter. The synthesis and the reports themselves should say that the research was commissioned by me, with my contacts. And every external post and artifact of mine should carry a block saying: this was made by Anton and Mycroft, because they were looking for such-and-such; they are available for hire; book a call; here is the calendar and the Linktree.

I still need to refine this logic a bit.

## How it connects

Both posts of the day are about the same thing: what an incoming piece of work needs before anyone acts on it.

The first one: a new voice message should not be acted on alone. Before setting a task, the robot pulls everything I said on the same topic from my voice notes and the vault. The second one: a deep research question should not rely on a single model either. It goes to 6 LLMs, the session moves on at a quorum of 4, and the late reports are still collected later instead of being dropped.

In both cases the rule is the same shape: gather more than one source before acting, and do not lose the sources that arrive late. The voice message needs its history; the research needs its quorum and its latecomers.
