## How it connects

One post so far today, and it is a method post rather than a result post: the result does not exist yet, which is the point of it.

The file in question is the one every session reads before doing anything. That gives it a property most files do not have: its size and shape are a standing tax on every single run, so a bad structure is not a one-off annoyance but a recurring cost. The usual reflex with a file like that is to keep trimming it by feel, which is exactly what is being paused here.

The move worth copying is the ordering. Research first, nothing applied, options laid out side by side, and only then a comparison with what we already do. The alternative, rewriting by taste and finding out in three weeks what broke, is cheaper to start and much more expensive to finish.

The second half matters as much as the first: whatever comes out of it is meant to go back out as a skill or a process for other people with the same problem, not to stay as a private tweak. Reading how others solved it creates an obligation to publish how we solved it.
