# I make my AI smarter every day, and I never trust one model blindly

I make my AI smarter every day.

First and most important: it has to remember everything we talked about.

Second. I do not let myself blindly trust a single model. It was trained on some set of data, it can be biased, it can not know something, it can be wrong somewhere, and so on. One head, even a very smart one, is only ONE point of view. The important things I run not through one model but through several at once. Grok, Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT. Each digs in its own way. Then I ask one of them to pull all the conclusions into a single synthesis. Where they agreed, where they diverged. And that is what I look at myself.

The AI brings the material and the options. It ADVISES me, but the decision is mine.

If you do it differently, how? What else do you use for deep research besides these four heroes?
