### "I'll check whether I've already done a DR on Apify" - no, and their own store reports three different sizes

He is looking into Apify - tools for agents, mostly for parsing and scraping. The plan: check whether a deep research on Apify already exists and, if not, gather his cases and commission one. Then look at what scrapers they have - YouTube, Twitter/X, TikTok and so on - and what people use them for: lead generation, machine learning, market research, competitor monitoring. And finally, think about how to use Apify in his daily work.

The first item is answerable in one query, and the fourth one has an answer he already owns.

**No, there is no deep research on Apify.** The registry holds not a single entry for it. The nearest neighbours are about other things: a July study of poisoned knowledge in mined alpha, an August one on which vendor DR modes can be driven from CLI without a browser, a September study on screens and children. Scraping as a market has never been commissioned. So the "if not" branch of his own sentence is the live one.

**What they have, counted from their public API today:**

| search | actors returned |
|---|---|
| linkedin | **10,110** |
| youtube | 5,975 |
| twitter | 4,676 |
| instagram | 4,384 |
| tiktok | 4,089 |
| facebook | 3,479 |

So the three platforms he named are all covered several thousand times over, and the platform with the most tooling is one he did not name.

**But the size of the store depends entirely on which surface you ask.** Four numbers, all from Apify or about it:

- their Store page advertises **70,000+** actors
- their API's `total` field returned **121,467**, then **122,787** a few minutes later, then **122,784**, then **122,787** again
- the same API stops returning records at **13,706**
- a third-party tracker counted **25,787** in May 2026, and a scraper listing cited 21,000+ earlier in the year

The gap that matters is the middle one: the API says a hundred and twenty thousand and hands over thirteen thousand seven hundred and six. I found the edge by binary search - at offset 13,705 a record comes back, at 13,706 nothing does, and nothing does at any offset above it either.

⚠️ There is an innocent explanation and I cannot rule it out: many APIs cap how deep you may page, and the cap here could simply be 13,706 records rather than the catalogue being that size. What is not explainable that way is `total` moving by 1,320 between two requests made minutes apart, while the reachable set stays fixed. Either way, the practical number for anyone building against it is 13,706, because that is what comes out.

**And the actor count points the opposite way from the user count.** The most-used actors on the store, by their own 30-day figures:

| actor | users in 30 days |
|---|---|
| `apify/instagram-scraper` | **69,289** |
| `compass/crawler-google-places` | 47,765 |
| `apify/instagram-profile-scraper` | 44,839 |
| `clockworks/tiktok-scraper` | 36,425 |
| `apify/instagram-reel-scraper` | 27,667 |
| `apify/google-search-scraper` | 27,142 |
| `streamers/youtube-scraper` | 17,966 |
| `apify/website-content-crawler` | 11,992 (free) |

LinkedIn has the most actors on the store - 10,110 - and its best-placed entry in that top list serves 886 people. Instagram has fewer than half as many actors and three of them carry 142,000 users between them.

So "what scrapers do they have" and "what do people actually use" are two different questions with two different answers. Counting listings tells you where supply piled up; counting users tells you where the work is. On this store those point in opposite directions.

**The pricing has just changed shape, which matters before any plan.** Apify stopped accepting new rental actors on 1 April 2026 and retired rental pricing entirely on 1 October, moving everything to pay-per-usage. About 72% of actors now bill pay-per-event on top of platform compute - in one 476-listing sample, 466 did. The free tier is a $5 monthly credit that does not roll over. Plan prices disagree between sources: Starter at $19 in one explainer and $29 in another, with Scale at $199 and Business at $999 in both.

So the cost model is per-event plus compute, the free allowance is five dollars a month, and anything built on last year's rental assumptions is built on a model that no longer exists.

**"How to use Apify in my daily tasks" - he already has twenty-six tools doing that job.** Apify appears nowhere in our setup: no API key in the secrets store, no mention in any of 280 skills, nothing in the scripts, nothing in the curated vault. What does exist, by name: `telegram-watch`, `mine-channel`, `watch-channel`, `fb-watch`, `x-inbox-watch`, `contrib-watch`, `cofounder-watch`, `content-mine`, `local-advisor-mine`, `photo-leads`, `graph-the-network`, `dig-through-data-brokers`, `find-person`, `find-anyone`, `read-deleted-pages`, `track-planes-and-ships`, and ten more.

That changes the question. Not "how do I add Apify to my day", but "which of these twenty-six does Apify replace more cheaply, and which does it not touch." The honest candidates are the platforms where we have no rail at all - TikTok, where there is no account and no tooling, and YouTube beyond the one history pipeline. The places where our own skills already run on live logged-in sessions are the places where a pay-per-event scraper competes against something that currently costs nothing.

⚠️ One more thing worth saying plainly: Apify came up three days ago in the answer to his own question. The TikTok commission of 13 November asked whether MCPs, bots or filters exist on top of social networks, and the answer named Apify as one of the two read-side servers. The information he is now looking for was already in the write-up of his previous post.

### "I need to finally redo the Palo Alto AI Lab site" - it was redone four months ago, on purpose, to do the opposite

He wants to get into SEO and GEO to promote the lab and the community. He spoke to someone from HeroicRankings.com and wants to study their approach. He positions the lab as a community of AI engineers, AI-native builders and power users; he lists the themes to build visibility around; he wants to know what SEO and GEO consist of, which routines to build, how to tie it to articles, GitHub, events and social, and how to earn backlinks. Then: *"I need to finally redo the Palo Alto AI Lab site. It should look like a modern, full community of AI engineers and AI-native builders."* And the site should say, in his words: build AI tools or harnesses - we have an audience that will test them; have an AI product - we can present it; strong engineer - become a speaker; want to be an AI-native power user - come to the calls.

The agency is real and does exactly what he described. Everything else here has history.

**The site already exists, and it sells something else.** Read live today:

| element | what is there now |
|---|---|
| title | Palo Alto AI Lab \| Independent multi-agent orchestration and agent governance research |
| description | Independent research lab working on multi-agent orchestration, agent governance, and zero-knowledge methods. Open artifacts, published evals, named authors. |
| h1 | Palo Alto AI Lab: the fleet experiment |
| structure | 8 h2, 11 h3, 43 links, JSON-LD present, canonical present |

And the words he wants it to rank for are not on the front page at all. Counted in the served HTML: **"meetup" 0, "community" 0, "speaker" 0, "MCP" 0, "harness" 0, "AI-native" 0, "power user" 0.** "Engineer" appears twice, "builder" once.

So the gap is not that the site looks dated. It is that the site is selling an independent research lab - orchestration, governance, zero-knowledge, published evals - and he wants it to sell a community.

**And that positioning was a decision, not an accident.** A Decision Memo from 23 July, still marked active, titled "palo-alto.ai → text-only research-lab site as hiring/acqui-hire magnet". It was built on a deep research with 467 sources, and its target reader is named explicitly: a recruiter, research lead or acqui-hire scout at a frontier lab, Anthropic first.

That memo cut, by name, four of the five things he now wants the site to say:

| he wants now | the memo's verdict in July |
|---|---|
| "we have an audience that will test your tools" | hero "I want you AI BUILDER" + book-a-call → **CUT**, "a sales funnel, wrong audience" |
| "have an AI product - we can present it" | "We enable Founders/VCs/Angels" → **CUT**, "sales" |
| "come to the calls, tell us your tasks" | "book a call" listed as a **genre violation** that cheapens the page |
| "strong engineer - become a speaker" | "we are looking for co-founders" → rewrite into a real Team page with named people and links |

The memo's whole thesis was that trust on that kind of page comes from traceability and restraint - verifiable anchors, dated activity, named authors - and that partner logo strips, "trusted by" and "book a call" read as hype to the exact insider he is trying to attract.

None of that means he is wrong now. Goals move, and a community funnel is a legitimate thing to want. But the site is not un-redone; it was redone deliberately four months ago to be the opposite of what he is describing, and the two cannot both be true on one front page. That is a decision to make, not a redesign to order.

**"I want to understand what SEO and GEO consist of" has four applied studies behind it.** From the registry:

- `DR26-06-30-HUB-01` - Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Content Strategy 2025-2026, **applied**, closed by a Decision Memo of its own
- `DR26-07-27-ZB-03-2306` - SEO+GEO strategy per platform: what to write where so humans find it and LLMs cite it, platform map, canonical and duplicates, **applied**
- `DR26-08-04-ZB-09-1803` - **a step-by-step protocol for getting into AI-engine answers for a small independent lab with zero backlink mass**, applied
- `DR26-08-04-ZB-08-1744` - GEO analytics from origin logs, and whether an open tool exists, **applied**

The third one is, almost word for word, the last sentence of his post. Plus four distilled insight notes and two Decision Memos, including a platform map from 27 July.

**And the pipeline is already on the shelf.** Thirteen skills, by name: `/geo`, `/audit-website-aeo`, `/improve-aeo-geo`, `/geo-content-research`, `/geo-content-planning`, `/write-seo-geo-content`, `/create-geo-charts`, `/build-backlinks`, `/build-resource-pages`, `/research-brand`, `/research-keywords`, `/audit-content`, `/reddit-opportunity-research`.

Audit the site, research the brand, research the keywords, plan the content, write it, build the resource pages, hunt the backlinks, chart the result. Every stage he described as "which routines do I need to build" has a skill with that stage's name on it.

⚠️ So the honest shape of this commission is not "learn SEO and GEO". It is: four studies and thirteen skills exist, and the lab's visibility is still where it is - which makes the open question whether the pipeline was ever run end to end, not whether it exists.

⚠️ One claim in the post I could not verify and one I can measure. The meetups "several times a week" I could not check from this machine - the public Luma addresses answer with redirects and need a logged-in session. The "grateful audience" was measured three days ago for a different post: 176,992 subscriptions across Telegram and X delivering about 300 readers per post, with the 144,878-subscriber English channel reaching 64. An SEO strategy aimed at growing that audience starts from a reach problem, not from a traffic problem.

⚠️ And the agency checks out. HeroicRankings.com is live, titles itself "SEO, GEO & Link Building Agency", leads with "B2B SEO Agency for Google and AI Search", and claims citation work across 30+ languages. Nothing about the conversation he described is off.

### How the two posts connect

Both posts of 16 November ask for research into something we already own, and in both cases the thing we own is one query away.

**Post 1**: is there already a DR on Apify? No - and that was the one item in the commission with a clean negative answer. Everything else about the store measured out in an afternoon, including a 8.9x inconsistency in the vendor's own numbers.

**Post 2**: what are SEO and GEO, which routines do I need, and the site needs redoing. Four applied studies, two Decision Memos, four insight notes and thirteen named skills already exist - and the site was deliberately rebuilt on 23 July, under an active memo, to be the opposite of what this post proposes.

Put together they draw the line of the week from the other side. The 13 November posts all read a dial that measured the wrong thing: a name instead of a tool, stars instead of merged PRs, subscribers instead of readers, memory instead of a contributor list. These two read no dial at all - they commission research in a direction where the research is already filed.

The distinction matters because the fixes are different. A wrong dial is fixed by measuring something else. An unread archive is fixed by looking before commissioning, and the registry is one grep.

And there is a sharper version of it in post 2. The commission asks which routines to build; thirteen skills with those exact stage names are on the shelf. So the live question is not whether the pipeline exists but whether it was ever run end to end - which is the same question post 1 raised about Apify from the other direction: twenty-six acquisition skills exist, none of them uses it, and the honest comparison is per-lane cost, not "should we adopt."

Both posts, in the end, are about an inventory nobody read.
