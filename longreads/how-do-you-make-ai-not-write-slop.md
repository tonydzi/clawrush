# What do you put in the brief so your AI does not write AI-slop?

I want to build a skill: anti-AI-slop. A skill that takes text written by a robot and makes it more human. It sort of makes it WORSE in a human way: strips the extra punctuation, kills the emoji, kills the little dashes, drops the fancy letters, cuts the text way down. I like writing very long things myself, and the people who read me have no time. They need it short, to the point, to the substance.

I need to build this skill and first do deep research: how to write so the text looks human. So that even if a robot wrote it, it is not bloated. The robot wrote too much, we cut. Robots love adding emoji and other junk, we remove all of it. We make the content look like a very busy person wrote it, someone with no time to write, no time to place every comma, and who slips up now and then. And another important thing, add humanity to posts: more of the events that actually happen in a person's life. Though that part does not always work, the examples do not always come to mind.

And I often write by voice. What goes into the posts is the transcript.
