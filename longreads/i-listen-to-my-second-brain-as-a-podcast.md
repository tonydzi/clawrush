# I listen to my own second brain as a podcast

Do you like NotebookLM? I do. Have for a long time. Right now I am using its podcast generation feature. My second brain holds a pile of notes, summaries, transcripts, deep-research reports. There is no time to reread all of it. But there is time to re-listen.

I take a chunk of the material and feed it into NotebookLM. It generates a podcast (if you have not tried it, definitely do). Two hosts start discussing my ideas, my notes. And I listen from the side to a podcast about my own knowledge. I think it over, I decide things about it, new ideas get born. And everything that would have sat as dead weight, that I would never have found the time to reread, now gets re-listened to by me.
