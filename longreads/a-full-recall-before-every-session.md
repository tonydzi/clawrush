# A full recall before every session, or the work does not start

One more of my hard rules for working with my AI.

Every time I start a session in Claude, I ask for a full recall, a full analysis of everything sitting in our brain. The session basically does not begin until a full analysis has run of everything relevant to it, across our whole second brain, across our whole Obsidian. I think this is right. What is the point of starting work without first finding everything that relates to the topic of that work? This is one of the most important steps in working with Claude: always do a full analysis of everything you have already done on this topic, and only then keep working.
