## What went out today

### Which tools build a talking-avatar content factory that also ships?

This one is a question to my readers.

I want to build a content factory that makes video content with me, for YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Long videos first: 5, 10, 15 minutes. Probably not through Higgsfield, but through, and here I am stuck, I forgot the name of that popular site where you upload your own video and it then generates content with you. Not Kling either, something else.

So the question is: which sites or LLMs are good for making video content with yourself? I record some amount of voice and text with my own face, and out of that a talking avatar gets built.

The second half matters more than the first. All of it has to be cut into shorts for TikTok, YouTube and Instagram, and then actually distributed, not just cut. And I do not need a farm of accounts for that: spreading it from my one ordinary account to the three platforms is enough.

If you know the tool I am trying to remember, or a better one, tell me.

### Sessions in Claude Code can hand work to each other

Did you know that when you run many sessions, say in Claude Code (I do not know how it works in Codex), one session can interact with another one?

One session does a piece of work and hands something over to another session. And you literally watch the second one receive it and start executing.

So communication between sessions is possible: one prepares something, another takes it and carries on with it.

I do not know whether you have tried this or not, but it is cool. Sessions in Claude Code can interact with each other and pass information between themselves.

By the way, I should run a deep research for myself on how this thing works, what its use cases are, and think about using it more actively.

### Four Telegram accounts, one vault

I want to sort out every conversation I have in Telegram. I have 4 accounts, and everything from them has to be pulled into my vault.

The conversations come in kinds. One-on-one chats: everything, from the very first day of those accounts, parsed, written out, put into the vault and linked up. My CRM is built around Telegram, where one dialogue with a person is already a card, so every dialogue belongs on the matching card. Small groups under 20 people: intro groups, collaboration groups from working with bloggers, launchpads, exchanges. Those get filed with the people who are in them. VIP groups where engineers or investors actually talk, somewhere between 50-100 people and fifteen hundred, not crypto rooms waiting for to the moon. And junk, which I should simply leave.

I only leave other people's groups. If a group is mine, I never leave it.

A few communities, Sostav and Lobsters and the like, I will work through separately, probably one session each.

## How it connects

All three posts of the day are about work that spans more than one place, and about where the seam sits.

The first asks for a factory that carries a video from my face and voice all the way to three platforms without a human moving files between steps. The seam there is between cutting and publishing, and today it is a person.

The second notices the same shape inside the tooling: sessions in Claude Code can hand work to each other, so a long job does not have to live inside one session. The seam is the handover itself, and it counts only when the receiving side does something with what it got.

The third is the same problem pointed at my own history. Four Telegram accounts hold years of conversations, and the vault does not have them. Pulling them across is the easy half; the seam is the sorting, because deciding which room is a real investor community and which is junk is a judgement, not a filter. That is why the one hard rule in the post is about not deleting yourself out of your own rooms: automation that cannot tell whose group it is will happily leave the ones that matter.
