# My AI pulls the alpha out of every call, so nothing valuable drowns

How systematic are you? I wrote recently, replying to a comment, that I am still building my own system. I add it into my processes bit by bit. The processing of call results, for example.

After every call, with the team, with partners, with leads, I am left with a transcript. A pile of text with gold buried in it, insights, decisions, promises. The whole transcript flows into my second brain. Then a distillation pass runs, where the AI extracts only the alpha. In parallel it pulls out the promises and the steps that are tied to dates and times. The AI files everything into its homes: agreements go into the lead's card in my CRM, project insights go into the projects themselves, tasks go to the task list. Everything gets cross-linked. When a week later I look up that person or that topic, everything relevant is handed to me at once, as a distillation of what was saved earlier.

Seems like the weak point in this process is me. When I know about the agreements but fumble at the last moment.

My AI vs me: score 1 to 0.
