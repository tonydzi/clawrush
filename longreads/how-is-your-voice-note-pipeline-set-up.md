# How is your voice-note pipeline set up?

I record a huge number of voice notes. I have written about this before, and about the problem: a voice note turns into text and then it is forgotten. Not all of them, but most. What I want is for any voice note to turn into a task.

How it works now: the voice note gets transcribed and becomes a task right in the chat. What I want next: for every important voice note to immediately spawn its own separate session with my AI. And for the system to figure out on its own what the note is, a post, a task for Claude, an idea to think over, and so on, without my involvement. It is still just an idea, I need to sit down and work out how. But I like the direction. You speak your mind on the move, and the thought is not lost, it goes into the work.

I would be grateful for tips.
