## How it connects

One post so far on this date, and it closes a loop opened a week earlier.

The earlier post set the parameters of learning by audio: playback speed per language, replays for the hard parts, and above all a drive as the only attention slot with nothing competing in it. That was a claim about format. This one is the first concrete receipt for it: a term read about in research did not land, the same term packaged as a generated podcast did, and the understanding arrived in the car rather than at the desk.

Worth stating the limit plainly, because it is the interesting part. Nothing here says audio is a better medium. It says the medium has to match the slot where attention is actually free, and reading had no such slot. A pipeline that emits text by default is optimising for the generator, not for the consumer, and the fix costs one conversion step.

The second half of the post is the same instinct pointed outward: the generated material is being moved into open access, and the setup that produces it is offered as a skill. Learning in private and publishing the method are two different acts, and only the second one leaves anything behind.
