### I want a full audit of every place I could publish. I have asked that question five times since July 2025, every previous one ended in a list of options, and meanwhile 332 posts went out across ten doors - none of them the places I keep naming

The ask, at 05:05 this morning: I need a proper full audit of every place I have ever found for publishing my scientific and non-scientific content. From LessWrong and Medium to, I don't know, journals and so on. Make a list of every place where anything of mine could in theory be published, and sort them: what content goes where, and how much effort each takes. On LessWrong, for instance, I could publish my daily content, but it needs human text there. Add Instagram, TikTok, Threads, Hugging Face, Kaggle and the rest - every possible place. Then sort: which places are useful to me and for what, where to concentrate and where not. Where publishing is easy, put a routine on it. Where it is hard, publish by hand. And I asked my co-founder to find in my vault every place we have already found, check what is already built and which routines already exist, and assemble the most complete, detailed table. Plus commission a deep research to sharpen the request.

That last part is the one I can actually answer, and the answer has two halves that do not sit well together.

#### This is the fifth time, and the four previous ones all ended the same way

My vault holds four decisions on exactly this question, and each one closes without a choice:

| when | the question | how it ended |
|---|---|---|
| 27 Jul 2025 | "Publications on AI: what exists and where to take it next" | *"the platform for the next paper was not chosen"* |
| 30 Jan 2026 | "Where to openly put 3-5 raw LLM researches a day" | *"no decision, only a collection of options"* |
| 16 Jun 2026 | "Content OS for turning Claude Code experience into posts" | *"recommended start: Telegram, Airtable, Make or n8n"* |
| 4 Jul 2026 | "Voice-to-content: 100% conversion of voice notes" | *"a deep research request was formulated, no concrete results"* |

Sixteen months, four rounds, each ending in a list - and three of the four ended by commissioning more research. Today's note ends the same way, with "commission a deep research to sharpen the request".

#### Meanwhile the answer was being written by work, not by a decision

While that question stayed open, a pipeline quietly grew to **332 cases and 2,364 placements** across **ten live doors**:

| door | placements | who presses the button |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook | 332 | by hand, mine |
| Telegram @PaloAltoAi (EN) | 307 | robot |
| Telegram @PaloAltoAiRu | 307 | robot |
| Telegram @ClawEng | 307 | robot |
| X | 273 | robot |
| Threads | 250 | by hand |
| dev.to | 181 | robot |
| Medium | 146 | by hand |
| GitHub longread | 128 | robot |
| GitHub devlog | 127 | robot |

**1,630 of those 2,364 placements - 69% - are already done by a robot.** My note says "where publishing is easy, put a routine on it; where it is hard, publish by hand". That is not a plan. That is a description of what already runs, and the line already sits exactly where the note would put it: the robot holds Telegram, X, dev.to and GitHub; the hand holds Facebook, Threads and Medium, which is 728 manual placements.

#### Of the eight places I name, two are already doing the work

| place I named | account | placements in my ledger |
|---|---|---|
| Medium | yes | **146** - already a working door |
| Threads | yes | **250** - already a working door |
| Hugging Face | **yes, `TonyDzi`** | 0 in the ledger - but see below |
| Kaggle | **yes, profile exists** | 0, and the profile looks empty |
| Instagram | cannot verify anonymously | 0 |
| TikTok | **no** - `tonydzz`, the exact handle of my YouTube channel, belongs to a stranger | 0 |
| LessWrong | **no** - 404 on three handles, nothing in their search | 0 |
| journals | ORCID and Scholar exist (h-index 6, ~120 citations) | 3 mentions in the tracker |

Two of the eight are already running. Two more exist and sit idle. One does not exist at all - and it is the one I open the note with.

#### And the idle one is the best result per unit of effort I have

Hugging Face has two datasets of mine, published 6 and 7 November: `agent-action-policy-eval` with **163 downloads** and `research-evidence-qa` with **76**. **239 downloads in nineteen days**, 2 followers, zero effort since upload.

For scale, from yesterday's measurement on the same account: a 42-minute podcast episode of mine has **4 views in sixteen days**. One dataset upload has out-performed it by a factor of forty, with no routine, no distribution, no teaser and no first comment.

And Hugging Face appears **nowhere** in my distribution tracker and **nowhere** in the pipeline config. The surface that reaches engineers best - which is goal number one - is the one my accounting does not know exists.

That is also the honest answer to "find in my vault every place we have already found". Hugging Face is in there: it is listed as a concept in that January 2026 note, ten months ago. It was found, it was never routed, and then it quietly produced the best numbers on the board.

#### So the audit is not the missing piece

The list of places is not what is missing - four lists already exist in the vault. What is missing is the step after the list: of everything found since July 2025, which ones were actually wired up. Two were (Medium, Threads, both now above a hundred placements). Two were not, and one of those is outperforming everything per unit of effort. One was never created. One has its name taken.

If I commission a fifth research round to "sharpen the request", I will get a fifth list. The thing that changed the numbers was never a list: it was wiring one more door into the pipeline. There are two doors already standing open and unwired, and the cheapest next move is to point the pipeline at them rather than to describe the landscape again.

⚠️ One honest limit: "placements in my ledger" counts what my own pipeline recorded, so a post published outside it does not appear. Instagram I could not verify at all without logging in, and the Kaggle profile is a single-page app that shows nothing to an anonymous reader - "looks empty" is what I could see, not what is there.

🧒 In plain words: I asked where I should publish my stuff. I have asked that four times before over the last year and a half, and every time it ended with a list and an order for more research. Meanwhile my robot and I quietly published 332 posts through ten different doors, and the robot does two thirds of that by itself. Of the eight places I named today, two are already among those ten doors. Two more I already have accounts on and never used - and one of them, Hugging Face, got 239 downloads in nineteen days with no effort at all, while a podcast of mine got four views. And LessWrong, the first place I named, I do not have an account on at all.
