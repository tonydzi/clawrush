## What went out today

### Four doors into Anthropic, and three are easier than the front one

My Claude always tells me: "If you want to find a job, find a referral guy inside the company."

And yesterday he sent me to a place where Anthropic events are happening everywhere. There were people from the Applied AI Team there, and I understood that yes, there is a chance of getting in. It is just hard. With Applied AI you land directly on the team.

There are easier options here: become an ambassador and run community events; become an SMB integrator and start selling Claude into SMB, that one is harder; ambassadors, SMB integrators and the Applied AI team.

In short, I am going to look for a chance to get in: you can write, look for the Chinese, write them letters. And also consider the various labs that want to move to the valley, they are needed too.

So far I am looking at Applied AI, forward deployed engineers, solution engineers, meetup runners, ambassadors, and all sorts of other things.

Because, it seems to me, you do not live on Anthropic alone. I need to look at what other options there are.

## How it connects

One post so far on this date, and it is the operational half of something that was only a definition three days earlier.

On 5 October a research pass produced the term forward deployed engineer, and the post about it was really about comprehension: the definition landed only after it was converted to audio. The role sat there as vocabulary. Here the same title reappears as a line in a list of things to actually apply for, next to solution engineer, ambassador and meetup runner. Learning a role name and treating it as a door are two different acts, and only the second one costs anything.

The structural point is worth stating on its own, because it generalises past this one company. **The front door is the one everybody counts, and it is usually the hardest.** Applied AI puts you directly on the team, which is exactly why it is contested. Ambassador, SMB integrator and meetup runner are adjacent surfaces of the same organisation, they are evaluated on different evidence, and all of them put you inside the room where the referral in the first line becomes possible. Counting four doors instead of one changes the arithmetic of the whole search.

There is also a refusal here that matters more than the enthusiasm: you do not live on Anthropic alone. A plan that depends on one employer is not a plan, and the labs relocating to the valley are named in the same breath for that reason.

## The numbers

One post so far on this date, and the only number in it is the count of doors: four named surfaces, three of them side entrances. That is a structural count, not a measurement.

Nothing here reports how many applications were sent, how many referrals exist, or what any conversion looks like. The event attendance is stated and the people from the Applied AI Team were met in person, which is evidence that the surface is reachable, not evidence of an outcome. The honest status is: a map of doors, no door opened yet.

Also not measured: whether a side entrance converts into the front one, or whether it becomes a career of its own. That is the thing the next few months answer.
